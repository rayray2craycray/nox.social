# Nox — Content Batch #1 (TikTok / IG Reels)

**Goal of every post:** get the right people to **join the waitlist at nox.social**.
**One rule:** the link lives in your bio; every post's job is to make someone *want* to tap it.
**Reality check:** all of these shoot on a phone. Relatable > polished. Post consistently > post perfectly.

---

## Account setup (do this first)
- **Handle:** `@nox.[city]` (e.g. `@nox.miami`) — city-specific reads as "for me," not "some app."
- **Name field:** `Nox · [City] Nightlife` (searchable keywords matter).
- **Bio:** `your night, optimized 🖤 | find the vibe, get rewarded for going out | [City] early access ↓`
- **Link:** `nox.social`
- **Pinned:** pin your best-performing hype post + the "what is Nox" explainer once you have them.

## Content principles (the stuff that actually moves views)
1. **Win the first 1 second.** Hook = on-screen text in frame one. If it's boring, they're gone.
2. **Vertical, loud text, captions on.** Most people watch on mute.
3. **Talk to *one* person** — a 22-year-old deciding where to go tonight.
4. **CTA is soft.** "link in bio for early access" at the end or in the caption — don't beg.
5. **Post 4–7×/week.** Volume is the strategy pre-launch; a few will pop, and those teach you what to make more of.
6. **Reply to every comment** in the first hour — it feeds the algorithm and builds the local community.

## The 12 posts

### 1 — The 5-group-chat chaos (relatable POV) 🔥 lead with this
- **Format:** POV / text-on-screen over a clip of you + friends getting ready.
- **Hook (frame 1):** "POV: it's 11pm and all 5 group chats have a different plan"
- **Shoot:** phone propped, you scrolling between chats looking overwhelmed, friends yelling suggestions.
- **Caption:** going out shouldn't need a group project 😭 we're fixing this. early access in [city] — link in bio 🖤
- **Audio:** trending "chaotic/relatable" sound (check the Trending tab).
- **Why:** relatability = shares = reach. This is your highest-ceiling concept.

### 2 — The dead-line regret
- **Format:** POV standing in a line.
- **Hook:** "waited 40 min for a line for THIS to be the vibe inside"
- **Shoot:** you in a line, then cut to an empty/dead room, disappointed face.
- **Caption:** imagine seeing the vibe *before* you leave the house. that's the whole idea. nox.social
- **Audio:** deflating/sad-trombone trend or a "let down" sound.

### 3 — The vibe map (feature tease)
- **Format:** screen-record the app's map/vibe (or a mockup) + your reaction.
- **Hook:** "a live map of which spots are actually popping tonight??"
- **Shoot:** screen recording of Discovery + vibe reads; you nodding/hyped.
- **Caption:** no more guessing. see the crowd, energy + wait before you go. [City] early access ↓
- **Audio:** hype/"wait it's real" sound.

### 4 — POV: you just hit Platinum (the status flex)
- **Format:** POV / aspirational.
- **Hook:** "POV: you go out enough that your favorite spot knows you by name"
- **Shoot:** you walking in confidently, screen flash of a tier badge (Guest→Regular→Platinum→Whale).
- **Caption:** nox rewards you for actually showing up. the more you go out, the higher your tier 🖤 show up. level up.
- **Audio:** confident/"main character" sound.
- **Why:** sells the retention hook as aspiration, not a feature.

### 5 — Build-in-public (founder, day 1)
- **Format:** talking to camera, raw.
- **Hook:** "i'm building the app nightlife actually needs and i'm doing it in public"
- **Shoot:** you, casual, 20–30 sec: the problem, what you're building, "follow the build."
- **Caption:** day 1. nox = find the vibe, get rewarded for going out, plan your night in one place. building it for [City] first. link in bio if you want in early.
- **Why:** build-in-public grows a loyal, rooting audience cheaply — perfect for bootstrapped.

### 6 — Early-access urgency
- **Format:** text-on-screen over a hype clip.
- **Hook:** "[City] — the first 100 on the list get in before anyone else"
- **Caption:** we're opening nox in [City] first. early access is capped. get on the list → nox.social 🖤
- **Audio:** countdown/urgent sound.

### 7 — "Rate the night out" green-screen / duet
- **Format:** green-screen react or duet a "worst night out" story.
- **Hook:** "you'd have known that spot was dead if you had this"
- **Caption:** we've all had the $40-cover-for-nothing night. never again. nox.social
- **Why:** duets/reacts borrow reach from existing viral nightlife content.

### 8 — Get rewarded for going out (core value, punchy)
- **Format:** fast cuts, big text.
- **Hook:** "what if going out actually gave you something back"
- **Shoot:** quick montage — walking in, tier badge, a perk/skip-line moment.
- **Caption:** loyalty points but for your nightlife. show up, climb tiers, get treated like a regular. early access ↓
- **Audio:** punchy hype beat (phonk/house).

### 9 — City engagement bait (comments = reach)
- **Format:** talking to camera or text-on-screen, a question.
- **Hook:** "[City]: where do we ACTUALLY go on a Thursday?"
- **Caption:** drop your spot 👇 building the [City] nightlife map and i want the real ones, not the tourist traps. nox.social
- **Why:** questions farm comments; comments = distribution. Also literally sources venues for you.

### 10 — The venue angle (intrigue / two-sided)
- **Format:** talking to camera, slightly insider.
- **Hook:** "we're telling venues exactly who's walking through their door — in real time"
- **Caption:** nox isn't just for people going out. venues finally see their crowd + who their regulars are. two-sided from day one. nox.social
- **Why:** signals ambition/legitimacy; good for reshares from industry folks.

### 11 — Aesthetic hype teaser (the "trailer")
- **Format:** 8–12 sec neon montage → logo → tagline.
- **Hook (visual):** fast night-out cuts (lights, crowd, skyline) on beat.
- **End card:** NOX · your night, optimized · [City] 2026 · nox.social
- **Caption:** the night finally has a home screen. [City], you're first. 🖤
- **Audio:** a hard drop / trending hype sound; cut on the beat.
- **Use:** pin this as your profile's first impression.

### 12 — Tester reaction (UGC / proof)
- **Format:** a real tester's clip or a screen-record of them using it.
- **Hook:** "first person outside the team just tried nox"
- **Caption:** real reactions from early testers in [City]. want in? link in bio. 🖤
- **Why:** social proof; once you have testers, this becomes a repeatable series.

## Posting cadence (first 2 weeks)
Aim for **5–6 posts/week.** A workable rotation:
- **Mon** relatable POV (#1, #2, #7)
- **Tue** feature tease (#3, #8)
- **Wed** engagement bait (#9) — post midday, reply all night
- **Thu** status/aspiration (#4) — Thursday = pre-weekend intent
- **Fri** hype/urgency (#6, #11) — people are deciding where to go
- **Sun** build-in-public / recap (#5, #12)

**Best post times for nightlife:** ~11am–1pm (planning) and ~6–9pm (pre-game). Friday/Saturday early evening is prime.
